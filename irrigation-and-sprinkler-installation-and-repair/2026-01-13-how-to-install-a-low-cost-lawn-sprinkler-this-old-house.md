Tools and Materials Needed to Install a Lawn Sprinkler

You’ll need the following items to install your lawn sprinkler: Garden hose Gravel

In-ground sprinkler head with quick-connect fitting

Protective ring and cover for the sprinkler head Shovel String or measuring tape Tarp Sprinkler Installation Process

Installing a low-cost lawn sprinkler is a straightforward process that you can complete in a few hours. Follow the steps below to make sure your sprinkler functions correctly. Determine Sprinkler Placement

Decide where to place your sprinkler based on the area you want to water and the potential obstacles. Use a long string or measuring tape to determine the area each sprinkler head can cover. Walk the perimeter of your lawn, and mark potential locations for sprinkler heads.

Place the sprinkler heads so that the area they cover overlaps slightly. This will lead to even watering. Dig the Hole

Use a shovel to dig a hole for the sprinkler head, and place the topsoil onto a tarp. Save the sod for later. Make the hole deep enough to accommodate the sprinkler head and allow for proper drainage. Install the Sprinkler Head

Hold the sprinkler head in place and partially backfill around it with the excavated topsoil. Add gravel around the sprinkler head and the male quick-connect fitting for improved drainage. Set the protective ring and cover over the male quick-connect fitting to protect it from debris and lawn mower damage. Securing your installation correctly prevents damage from external factors and keeps your system performing reliably. Finishing the Installation

Add more topsoil to the hole, and leave enough space for the sod you saved. Patch around the sprinkler head with the sod, and integrate it with the surrounding lawn. Attach the garden hose to the sprinkler head using the quick-connect fitting. Adjusting and Testing the Sprinkler

Once you’ve installed the sprinkler, adjust it and test it to make sure it performs correctly. Setting the Spray Range

Adjust the spray range to make sure your sprinkler covers the entire lawn. Turn on the water supply to activate the sprinkler. Observe the sprinkler’s spray pattern and its range. Adjust the plastic arms on the sprinkler head to increase or decrease the swing and coverage area. Fine-Tuning the Coverage

Walk around the area that the sprinkler covers to identify any dry spots or areas that you’ve overwatered. Make small adjustments to the sprinkler head as needed. Repeat the process for each sprinkler head that you’ve installed. This will reduce waste. Lawn Sprinkler Maintenance and Care
