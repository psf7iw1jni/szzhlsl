What to Consider When Choosing One of the Best Hardwood Floor Refinishing Companies and Contractors

Before hiring a hardwood flooring refinishing company, it’s important to know a few key components in the decision-making process. The following are some of the most critical considerations to keep in mind when comparing companies and choosing the best fit. Service Area and Scheduling

Hardwood flooring companies have service areas in which they operate. After all, they have to bring a lot of equipment to the site, so they need to be in relatively close proximity to their workshops. For this reason, some companies may only operate within a 50-, 75-, or 100-mile radius of their shop. It’s important for shoppers to find a company that services their address.

On top of finding a company whose service area works for them, customers will want to consider scheduling. Many refinishing contractors are very busy and could take months to get to the customer’s project. Making sure that the contractor’s schedule works with the homeowner’s is a vital piece of the puzzle, so it may take some shopping around. Estimate Scheduling

A hardwood flooring refinisher may be able to give a rough price for the project over the phone, but most want to see the project site before they offer an estimate. During this time, a company rep will show up on-site, take measurements, assess any challenges, and then give the customer a range of how much the project will cost. However, this involves scheduling a time that works for the homeowner.

To make this process easier, many hardwood flooring companies offer their customers the ability to schedule their estimates online. They can log in, choose a day and time that works for them, and then tailor their schedules to meet the time. Companies that don’t offer online scheduling will provide a phone number customers can call for phone scheduling. In either case, companies typically confirm within 48 hours of the requested appointment date. Warranty

The best hardwood flooring refinishing companies stand behind their work. They offer warranties ranging from 1 year to several years, giving homeowners peace of mind that the company will set it right if any issue arises.

Even with all of that background on the best hardwood floor refinishers, there may still be some questions that need nailing down when searching online for “floor refinisher near me.” The following are some of the most frequently asked questions about refinishing hardwood floors, so be sure to check for the answers to these questions listed below. Q. What is the best finish for floors?

There’s no one best finish for hardwood floors. The most popular finish for hardwood floors is satin. Satin has a nice shine but is also easy to keep clean. As the light disperses off of a satin finish, it doesn’t show all the dirt or imperfections in the floor, making it one of the best oak hardwood floor finishes and for other hardwoods, too. Q. Do refinished floors scratch easily?

Refinished hardwood floors are as durable as newly installed hardwood floors. While the wood planks may be thinner after sanding, the finish coat applied over the floor is the same.

Q. How long do refinished floors last on average?

Hardwood floors generally need refinishing every 8 to 12 years, so on average, once a decade. And hardwood floors can be refinished up to 10 times before needing replacement.

Q. Is it cheaper to refinish or replace hardwood floors?

It’s much cheaper to refinish hardwood floors than replace them. As long as extensive hardwood floor repairs aren’t necessary to refinish the existing floor, replacing hardwood floors almost always costs more.

Q. How much does it cost to refinish 1,000 square feet of hardwood floors?

Refinishing 1,000 square feet of hardwood flooring costs between $3,000 and $8,000. This includes the cost of sanding the existing floor, applying finish to the hardwood floor, and sealing.
