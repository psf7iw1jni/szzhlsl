Do you want to arrange your iPhone apps in alphabetical order? You can quickly reset your Home screen layout in your Settings or you can manually alphabetize your apps. iOS's "App Library" also provides an alphabetized list for easy access. This wikiHow will show you how to get your apps in alphabetical order on your iPhone 14, 13, 12, 11, SE, and older. Things You Should Know

Reset Home layout in "Settings" → "General" → "Transfer or Reset iPhone" → "Reset" → "Reset Home Screen Layout".[1]XResearch source

Manually organize apps by long-pressing, then dragging to a new location.

[iPhone Home Screen](https://iphone-home-screen.themaplelane.com/iphone-home-screen/20260826.html)

Swipe left to access the "App Library". Tap the search bar to see an alphabetized list. Steps Method 1 Method 1 of 3: Resetting Home Screen Layout 1 Open Settings

. This is the grey cog icon. Make sure to update your iPhone.[2]XResearch source

This method will reset all default apps to their default locations and alphabetize all third-party apps. This will also erase any existing app folders on your home screen.

If you don't want to lose unique folders, try manually organizing the apps instead. 2

Tap General. This can be towards the middle of Settings.[3]XResearch source Advertisement 3

Tap Transfer or Reset iPhone. You can find this towards the bottom, above Shut Down.[4]XResearch source 4 Tap Reset. A pop-up menu will open. 5

Tap Reset Home Screen Layout. This will reset your Home screen to its default settings with default apps. All third-party apps you have installed will be organized alphabetically.[5]XResearch source

[to Alphabetize Apps](https://to-alphabetize-apps.themaplelane.com/to-alphabetize-apps/20260826693.html)

If you're looking for new ways to organize your iPhone, consider an aesthetic Home screen layout. Advertisement Method 2 Method 2 of 3: Manually Organizing Apps 1

Tap and hold an app on your Home screen. If you added apps to your Home screen, you can easily organize them how you want.[6]XResearch source

Use this method to manually alphabetize your Home screen apps. 2

Drag the app to a new location. Doing so will put your Home screen in Edit mode, and the apps will shake.[7]XResearch source

You can also long-press an app and tap Edit Home Screen. 3

Release the app. This will place the app in the designated position. 4

Repeat for all remaining apps. Use steps 1 to 3 to manually place the apps in alphabetical order. 5

Click Done when finished. This will exit Edit mode.

Alternatively, click anywhere on the Home screen to exit Edit mode. Advertisement Method 3 Method 3 of 3: Using the App Library 1

Swipe left on your Home screen. If you have more than one page for your Home screen, continue to swipe until you see the App Library.[8]XResearch source

The App Library contains a list of pre-alphabetized apps. 2

Click the search bar at the top. Your apps will be displayed in alphabetical order.[9]XResearch source

Scroll up and down to view the apps, or use the letters listed on the right side to navigate. Advertisement Expert Q&A  Search Add New Question Ask a Question 200 characters left
