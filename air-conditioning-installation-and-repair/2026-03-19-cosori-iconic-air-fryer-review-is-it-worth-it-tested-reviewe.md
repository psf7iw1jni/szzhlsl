I tested the Cosori Iconic, a stainless steel 6.5-quart air fryer that combines multiple cooking modes with an eco-friendly design and a unique, sculpted aesthetic. After using the Iconic in my kitchen for 2 weeks, I can confidently say it’s an excellent choice for anyone looking to buy a mid-range air fryer. What sets the Cosori Iconic apart?

Cosori has used as little plastic as possible in the Iconic’s design, and the stainless steel is high-quality, corrosion-resistant, and durable. The one drawback of a stainless steel appliance is that it shows fingerprints and smudges easily, and I needed to wipe down the exterior regularly to prevent this.

The Iconic has six cooking functions: air fry, roast, bake, dry, proof, and reheat. Most people will buy this kitchen appliance for the air fry function, so that’s the one I tested the most. Over a couple of weeks of family meals, I made chicken nuggets (a staple for my kids), French fries, roasted veggies, and even a grilled cheese sandwich. The Iconic cooked these items much faster than expected, so I adjusted my cooking time to prevent burning. The food came out perfect: crispy and delicious, with no added oil.

I also used the reheat function several times. I usually reheat leftovers in my microwave, but that often results in a burning-hot exterior and a cold interior. The Iconic reheated my food evenly and quickly without burning or drying it out. How does the Cosori Iconic app work?

The Iconic connects to the VeSync app, where you can set cooking times, temperatures, and functions. You do need to press the start button on the air fryer physically—this is for safety, so you don’t accidentally turn it on when you don’t mean to. Connecting the Iconic to the app was super simple and took me less than two minutes.

The app has a cooking chart that shows recommended cooking times for a variety of foods, including hamburgers and hot dogs, ribs, salmon, broccoli, and fries. Once you select the food you want to cook, you can send the settings to the Iconic, so all you need to do is physically press start. This was a neat feature that I used several times. The Cosori Iconic is pricier than many air fryers. However, the Iconic has several features that cheaper air fryers lack, most notably its sustainable design and construction. If you’re trying to reduce plastic in your home or want to control your air fryer with an app, the Cosori Iconic is well worth the price—and it doesn’t hurt that it looks great on your kitchen counter.

The Cosori Iconic is a solid choice for anyone looking for an air fryer that will enhance their kitchen aesthetic. Its stainless steel construction not only looks good but also reduces the amount of plastic used in the appliance. The Iconic’s ceramic cooking surfaces are BPA-free and PFAS-free, adding to its eco-friendly qualities.

The Iconic cooks fries, fish, chicken, and veggies quickly, and the available app lets you send settings to the air fryer right from your phone (though you still need to press the start button). Cosori has included multiple recipes, both in a booklet and in the app, with the corresponding cooking settings to make dinner time easier.

Like most air fryers, the Iconic is quite loud. If you have an open kitchen and living room, expect to hear complaints from anyone trying to watch a movie while the Iconic is running. But since it cooks so quickly, the noise is short-lived. You’ll also need to wipe the Iconic down quite often; the stainless steel construction shows fingerprints and smudge marks immediately.

Ultimately, the Cosori Iconic is a top pick if you’re looking for a sustainable air fryer that enhances your dining experience and your kitchen’s aesthetics.
