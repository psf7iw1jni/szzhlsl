How to Write a Great First Message on a Dating App

Crafting the perfect first message can be tricky, but it’s your chance to make a great first impression and spark a conversation. The best messages feel authentic, friendly, and show you’ve taken a genuine interest in the other person. Here are some tips to keep in mind:

[on Dating App](https://on-dating-app.themaplelane.com/on-dating-app/20260826.html)

Keep your tone positive and approachable. Friendly messages invite open conversation and make the other person feel comfortable.

Use open-ended questions or comments. This encourages replies that go beyond yes/no answers and keep the chat flowing naturally.[1]XExpert SourceJaskaran Singh Dating CoachExpert Interview

Personalize your message by referencing something from their profile. It shows you’ve paid attention and aren’t sending a generic line.

Avoid overused greetings and clichés. Simple “Hey” or “What’s up?” messages usually get ignored.

Be clear but respectful about your intentions. Whether you want a casual chat or something more serious, honesty helps build trust.

Ultimately, your message doesn’t have to be perfect or super creative — it just needs to be sincere and invite a meaningful conversation.
